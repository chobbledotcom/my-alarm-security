Our MyAlarm Security van has recently been in for its annual MOT and vehicle check at Neves MOT Centre in Sidcup and we are very pleased to say it passed with flying colours!

As a local security company based in Sidcup, our van is a big part of our everyday working life. It’s out on the road every day, taking us to customers across Sidcup, Bexley, Orpington, Bromley, Greenwich, Dartford and the surrounding areas, whether we’re installing a new CCTV system or burglar alarm, servicing an existing security system or attending an alarm fault.

That’s why keeping our van properly maintained, regularly serviced and safe on the road is so important to us. A reliable van means we can reliably get to our customers, carrying all the tools, equipment and spare parts we need to get the job done.

A big thank you as always to the team at Neves MOT Centre in Sidcup for carrying out our annual MOT and giving the van a thorough check over. It’s always good to support other local Sidcup businesses, and even better to drive away knowing everything is safe, roadworthy and ready for another busy year ahead.
