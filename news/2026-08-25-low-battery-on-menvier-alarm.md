One of the most common calls we get is from customers saying, “my alarm keeps beeping and I can’t get in touch with the guy who used to service it.”

In most cases, it’s simply just your alarm letting you know that there is an issue or that a battery needs replacing.

Ignoring warning beeps or faults may lead to bigger problems and can leave your alarm system unable to protect your home when you need it most.

If your burglar alarm system has started beeping, is showing a fault, or hasn’t been serviced for a while and needs a battery replacement, we’d be more than happy to help.

We are a friendly, family-run business with over 28 years of experience. We provide burglar alarm servicing, repairs and installations across Sidcup, Bexley, Bromley, Orpington, Greenwich, Dartford and the surrounding areas, as well as CCTV installations. 

If you’re unsure what your alarm is trying to tell you, why it is beeping or what the fault is, get in touch with our team for some friendly and honest advice.
